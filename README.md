# Video Game Industry Sales Analysis

A business-focused data analysis of 16,598 video game titles (1980 to 2020), answering market questions for a fictional consultancy, GameVision Analytics, that advises publishers, investors and platform holders.

**[View the full notebook with all outputs](https://ayushramtekkar.github.io/video-game-sales-analysis/)**

## Business questions

1. Which genre generates the most global sales?
2. Which platform has the highest total sales?
3. Which publishers dominate the market, and how concentrated is it?
4. How have global sales evolved over time?
5. How do consumer preferences differ across North America, Europe and Japan?
6. Which individual games are the all-time best sellers?

## Key findings and recommendations

- **Action** is the largest genre by volume, especially in North America and Europe.
- PlayStation and Xbox suit Western-market titles; Nintendo platforms suit family-oriented and Japanese-market titles.
- Role-Playing games should be prioritised for Japan; Action and Sports for Western markets.
- Catalogue size does not reliably predict sales: franchise IP and platform fit matter more. The top 15 best sellers are almost all established franchises or platform-bundled titles.
- **Limitations:** physical retail units only, so digital and mobile sales (the majority of today's market) are missing and post-2010 figures understate the market.
